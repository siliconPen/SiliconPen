<h1>A Fresh Home for SiliconPen ✨</h1>
<p>If you’ve been reading my posts on Hashnode, welcome back — and if you’re new here, hi, I’m Rishi 👋</p>
<p>SiliconPen just moved into its <strong>own home</strong> at <a href="https://siliconpen.com">siliconpen.com</a>, and this is the first “official” post from the new setup.</p>
<p>I wanted to quickly share <strong>what changed, why I moved, and what might still look a bit work-in-progress</strong> while I keep tuning things.</p>
<hr class="section-divider">
<h2>Why move away from Hashnode?</h2>
<p>Hashnode was an amazing place to start.<br />It gave me:</p>
<ul>
<li>A clean editor to write in  </li>
<li>Built-in SEO and RSS  </li>
<li>A small but real audience to write for</li>
</ul>
<p>But after a while, I started feeling the itch to:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Control everything</strong> — layout, series, structure, weird experiments  </li>
<li>Make <strong>custom series pages</strong> (like <em>Rant Electronics</em>, <em>Oscillations</em>, <em>Pathway to Arduino</em>, <em>Real Circuits</em>, etc.)  </li>
<li>Build tools around my writing (like my own Markdown → HTML flow, TOC, and all the nerdy stuff you don’t usually see)</li>
</ul>
<p>So… I moved. Now every pixel you see here is wired by hand. If something is broken, it is <em>100% my fault</em> 😆</p>
<hr class="section-divider">
<h2>What’s new on the site?</h2>
<p>A few things you’ll notice across the new SiliconPen:</p>
<ul>
<li><p><strong>Custom article layout</strong><br />Clean typography, focused reading area, and a “On this page” sidebar for longer posts.</p>
</li>
<li><p><strong>Series pages</strong><br />Articles are grouped into series like:</p>
<ul>
<li><em>Rant Electronics</em> – chaotic, emotional electronics rants  </li>
<li><em>Oscillations</em> – weekly-ish tech + geopolitics analysis  </li>
<li><em>Pathway to Arduino</em> – structured Arduino learning path  </li>
<li><em>Real Circuits</em> – circuits that actually do something</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li><p><strong>Reference vault (Citadel)</strong><br />There’s now a dedicated <strong>References</strong> page where I list the sources that power articles, instead of hiding them in random links.</p>
</li>
<li><p><strong>Better control over performance &amp; SEO</strong><br />Lightweight, static, and handcrafted. No heavy frameworks doing surprise things in the background.</p>
</li>
</ul>
<hr class="section-divider">
<h2>Things still in progress 🛠️</h2>
<p>The site is <strong>new</strong> and I’m still tuning it. So if you spot something odd, that’s why. A few things that might be rough for a bit:</p>
<ul>
<li>Some older links from Google / Hashnode may <strong>redirect</strong> to the new paths  </li>
<li>A few visual details will keep changing (spacing, colors, tiny UI tweaks)  </li>
<li>Comments and some interactive parts may evolve as I test what works best</li>
</ul>
<p>If you hit a broken link, feel free to jump back to the <strong>Articles</strong> or <strong>Series</strong> page and you’ll probably find what you were looking for.  </p>
<p>I’m also still experimenting with where / how to place things like newsletters, references, and (eventually) ads in a way that doesn’t ruin the reading experience.</p>
<hr class="section-divider">
<h2>A small thank you to Hashnode</h2>
<p>Hashnode was where SiliconPen took its <strong>first steps</strong>.<br />I wrote my early articles there, learned how to structure posts, and met the first people who actually read my stuff.</p>
<p>So even though the content now lives here, a big <strong>thank you</strong> to everyone who ever read, liked, or commented on a Hashnode article. You’re part of why this standalone version exists.</p>
<hr class="section-divider">
<h2>What you can expect next</h2>
<p>Now that the “plumbing” is mostly done, I’ll be focusing on:</p>
<ul>
<li>More <strong>Rant Electronics</strong> episodes  </li>
<li>Continuing the <strong>Oscillations</strong> series  </li>
<li>Deep dives on <strong>real circuits</strong>, not just equations  </li>
<li>The full <strong>Pathway to Arduino</strong> roadmap</li>
</ul>
<p>If you find bugs, broken links, layout glitches, or have ideas, you can always ping me or just mentally tag it as <em>“site under construction, but vibes are good”</em>.</p>
<p>Thanks for sticking around through the migration.<br />This is just the beginning of <strong>SiliconPen 2.0</strong> 🖊️⚡</p>
